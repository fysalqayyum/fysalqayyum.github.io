Services

How I can help

Four areas, one person who does the work with you: moving to Germany or the Gulf, finishing and publishing research, simulation and materials consulting, and training for research groups. Every engagement starts with a conversation about your situation, not a package.

Careers & Mobility

For researchers planning a move to Germany or to the Gulf.

PhD, Postdoc & Research Careers in Germany Route choice, finding groups, German application documents and the first year. Academic Jobs in Saudi Arabia & the Gulf Where to apply, documents for Gulf committees, interviews, offers and your first months.

Finish & Publish

For PhD students and researchers whose project, paper or proposal has stalled.

PhD & Research Rescue Stalled PhD, supervisor conflict, authorship dispute or a paper that keeps coming back. Scientific Writing Coaching For results that are ready when the paper is not: structure, argument, reviewer responses and a writing routine. PhD & Research Mentoring An independent second opinion on your thesis, your research plan and your next career step. Grant Proposal Consulting Narrative, work plan and reviewer-facing language for research funding proposals.

Simulation & Materials

For research groups and industrial teams who need hands-on help with models and data.

Crystal Plasticity Simulation (DAMASK) EBSD-based RVEs, calibration and local stress-strain results that hold up in review. Metal Forming FEM (ABAQUS) Springback, yield-function calibration and forming defects in ABAQUS simulations. Phase Field Simulation Recrystallization, spheroidization, grain growth and solidification, calibrated against micrographs. EBSD Data Analysis & MTEX Scripted MTEX and Python analysis, from measurement review to figures and simulation inputs. Materials Failure Analysis Fracture surface, microstructure and loading history read together to find why a part failed. Mechanical Test Data Analysis Tensile, fatigue, hardness and impact data connected to the mechanism behind them. Process–Structure–Property Analysis Linking processing to microstructure to properties, so you know how to get something different. Multiscale Modeling Strategy Scale selection, tool matching and validation design before you build models that never connect.

Training & Workshops

For groups and institutes who want their own people to learn the method.

Custom Courses & Workshops Workshops on scientific writing, AI for research, DAMASK and MTEX, built around your group’s own data.

How it works

  1. Tell me what’s going on. A few lines on your situation, your deadline and what you have tried. I reply within two working days.
  2. Roadmap session. A 60-minute video call about your case. For labs and companies, this is a scoping call.
  3. Written plan. Within five working days you get a plan, or for projects a written scope with a fixed quote.
  4. Your choice. Carry out the plan on your own, or continue with me through a review, mentoring or a project.
