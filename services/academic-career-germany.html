PhD, Postdoc & Research Careers in Germany: Coaching

For students, researchers and engineers from South Asia, the Gulf and elsewhere who want a PhD, a postdoc, a TV-L 13 research post or an industry job in Germany. I spent nine years in German research and earned my Dr.-Ing. there, and I coach you from where you are to an offer.

Who this is for

This is for people who are serious about a research or engineering career in Germany and want a plan instead of a hundred unanswered applications. Typical clients are master’s graduates looking for a funded PhD post, PhDs looking for a postdoc, and experienced engineers weighing a research post against an industry job.

What I do and don’t do

  • This is career coaching, not placement. I don’t place candidates, and I take no fee that depends on you being hired.
  • Advice on your individual residence or visa case is a regulated legal service in Germany. I point you to Make it in Germany, the Federal Foreign Office and anabin, and to a qualified lawyer for your own case.
  • I don’t quote salary scales or visa thresholds here, because they change every year. Check the official sources for the current figures.
  • I don’t write your applications for you. I review, explain and improve them with you.

Common questions

Should I do a PhD, a postdoc or go into industry in Germany?

It depends on what you want in five years, not on which offer comes first. A PhD or postdoc builds a research career but usually on fixed-term contracts; industry pays more sooner but values different evidence. We compare the routes against your profile and goals before you apply anywhere.

How do I find a supervisor or research group in Germany?

Most research posts in Germany are attached to a professor’s group and a funded project, so you find them through the groups as much as through job portals. I help you build a target list and write emails that professors actually answer.

Is my degree recognised in Germany?

Check it in the anabin database of the Kultusministerkonferenz, which lists foreign institutions and degrees and how they are rated. I help you understand what the entry means for your applications; formal recognition decisions come from the responsible German authorities.

Can you help with my visa or Blue Card?

I can explain the general routes and point you to the official sources, such as Make it in Germany and the German Federal Foreign Office. Advice on your individual residence case is a regulated legal service in Germany, so for that you need a qualified lawyer or the responsible authority.

Can you find me a position?

No. I coach you through the process, but I don’t place candidates, and nothing I charge depends on whether you are hired. What I can do is make sure your target list, documents and interviews are as strong as they can be.
