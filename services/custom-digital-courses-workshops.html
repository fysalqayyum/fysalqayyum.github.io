How it works

  1. 15-minute call. Describe your team, their current level, and what capability or outcome the workshop should build.
  2. Scoped design. Single-session workshops, multi-day series, or ongoing training cadence — format and content agreed before scheduling.
  3. Delivery. Live sessions (remote or in-person), built around your team's own software versions and, where possible, their own data.

Common questions

Can a workshop use our own data instead of examples?

Yes, and it is the more effective format — agreed during scoping so the content is built around what your team will actually use afterward.

Do you offer single sessions or only full courses?

Both. A single focused session (e.g. "DAMASK RVE construction from your EBSD data") is common, as is a multi-day onboarding series for new team members.

Is remote delivery available?

Yes — most workshops run remotely; in-person delivery is possible for larger engagements where travel is warranted.

How is this different from your free YouTube content?

The YouTube library is free and general. A commissioned workshop is built around your team's specific software, materials, and questions, with live interaction and follow-up.

Can you run the scientific writing workshop for a whole department?

Yes — the group format based on "How to Write a Lot" and the Writing Club model scales to department size; see scientific writing coaching for the individual-coaching version of the same material.
